## Printing it

```sh
sed -n '/^```text$/,/^```$/p' docs/banner.md | sed '1d;$d'
```

`yuna-welcome` uses the compact `YUNA` wordmark in
`distro/airootfs/usr/share/yuna/logo.txt` instead: fastfetch renders the logo
next to system information, and the portrait is wider than the info column.
